# Dictation and Read-Aloud

Last updated: 2026-11-25

If your administrator has turned them on, Atlas can type what you say and
read replies to you. The two features are separate; you may have one, both
or neither.

## Dictating a prompt

1. Click the **microphone** button next to the message box. The first time,
   your browser asks for permission to use the microphone.
2. Speak. The button turns into a red **stop** button with a timer, and a
   **cancel** (X) button appears beside it.
3. Click **stop** to finish. Atlas transcribes the recording and puts the
   text into the message box at the cursor.
4. Read it over, fix anything that was misheard, add to it, and press
   **Send** when you are ready. Dictation never sends a message for you.

Click **cancel** instead of stop to throw the recording away: nothing is
transcribed or inserted. Recording stops by itself at the length limit your
administrator set and transcribes what was recorded up to then. Starting a new
chat or opening another conversation while you are recording, or while a
recording is being transcribed, cancels it, so what you said is not put into a
different conversation's message box.

If the microphone button says access is blocked, allow the microphone for
the site in your browser's site settings (usually the icon at the left of
the address bar) and try again.

## Having a reply read aloud

1. Hover over a finished assistant reply and click the **speaker** button in
   its header, next to Copy.
2. After a moment the audio starts, and the button becomes **pause**,
   **stop** and a **speed** menu (0.75x to 2x). The speed you pick is
   remembered.
3. Starting another reply stops the one that is playing.

Code blocks are skipped rather than read character by character. A very long
reply is read up to the limit your administrator set, and Atlas tells you
when it has shortened one.

## Showing or hiding the buttons

Open **Settings**, go to **General**, and find **Voice**. There is one
switch for the microphone button and one for the read-aloud button. Until
you change a switch it follows your administrator's default. Your choice is
saved in this browser.

A switch only appears for a feature your administrator has made available to
you. If neither is available, the Voice section is not shown.
