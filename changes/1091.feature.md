The chat-history PostgreSQL connection pool is now configurable (`CHAT_HISTORY_DB_POOL_SIZE`, `CHAT_HISTORY_DB_MAX_OVERFLOW`, `CHAT_HISTORY_DB_POOL_TIMEOUT`, `CHAT_HISTORY_DB_POOL_RECYCLE`, `CHAT_HISTORY_DB_POOL_PRE_PING`, `CHAT_HISTORY_DB_CONNECT_TIMEOUT`, `CHAT_HISTORY_DB_DISABLE_POOLING`), so multi-replica deployments can keep total connections within the server's budget or pool through PgBouncer. Defaults keep the previous 5 + 10 pool, now with a 30-minute connection recycle and a 10-second connect timeout; the chat-history guide documents the replica connection budget.
