Saved conversations now record the data classification they were created under, and the server refuses to restore, reopen or continue one under a different compliance level, including after a reconnect, in parallel runs and through direct API calls. Conversations saved before this release have no record and cannot be continued while compliance levels are enforced. They stay listed and exportable, and an operator can record a level for them with `scripts/stamp_conversation_classification.py` (see docs/admin/compliance.md).
