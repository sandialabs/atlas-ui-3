Team prompt library: save versioned system prompts, share them with Atlas groups (view/use) or named people (use, edit, manage), fork them, and pick a specific or latest version in chat. Shared prompts are resolved and authorized server-side on every turn, each conversation keeps the exact version it ran with, and every change is audited (`/api/prompts`, migration `004`). Concurrent edits and sharing changes are guarded by revisions (HTTP 409 instead of lost updates), and an unavailable pinned prompt can be cleared by the conversation owner (`DELETE /api/prompts/pins/{conversation_id}`). Clearing a pin also applies to sessions that already have the conversation open, a conversation that fails to load is refused rather than run on another prompt, save-then-publish publishes exactly the saved draft, and a turn naming both a team prompt and a custom prompt or persona is rejected. Pin changes are compare-and-set on a pin revision, so two windows cannot silently overwrite each other's team prompt; version history is paged; and authorization checks for listings and sharing targets are bounded and fail closed. `conversation_saved` reports the pin that was actually stored, pin-conflict errors name their conversation and only affect it, archived entries keep read-only version history and export, sharing is limited to configured groups (503 when membership cannot be checked), listings no longer load prompt text, and the picker and library panel handle loading, failed requests and stale search responses without losing a selection or unsaved edits. A conversation with damaged prompt settings can be recovered by its owner's `DELETE /api/prompts/pins/{conversation_id}` (no pin is guessed; classification and other settings are kept, or stay invalid when nothing could be read), the library panel drops responses for a previous search the moment the filter changes, and the Mine and Shared listings no longer wait on group checks they do not need.
